CROP: WHEAT (GANDUM) - PAKISTAN AGRICULTURAL GUIDE

1. Sowing Time (Waqt-e-Kasht):
- Optimal sowing time in Punjab and Sindh is from November 1 to November 20.
- Late sowing after December 10 reduces yield by 1% to 1.5% per day.

2. Seed Rate and Varieties (Beej aur Iqsam):
- Recommended seed rate for timely sowing is 40 to 50 kg per acre.
- For late sowing (December), increase seed rate to 50 to 60 kg per acre.
- Approved varieties include Akbar-2019, Dilkash-2020, Subhani-2021, and Arooj-2022.

3. Fertilizer Management (Khadon ka Istimal):
- Apply 1.5 to 2 bags of DAP and 1 bag of SOP/MOP at the time of land preparation.
- Apply 2 to 2.5 bags of Urea in two split doses with first and second irrigation.

4. Irrigation Schedule (Aabpashi):
- First irrigation (Kor) should be applied 20 to 25 days after sowing (Crown Root Initiation stage).
- Critical stages for water are Tillering, Booting, and Grain Filling.
- Total 4 to 5 irrigations are required depending on rainfall.

5. Harvesting (Katai):
- Harvest when grain moisture drops below 12% to 14% and stalks turn golden yellow.
