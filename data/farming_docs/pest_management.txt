INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT (IPM) - MAJOR CROPS

1. Wheat Pests and Diseases:
- Rust (Kungi): Yellow and brown rust appear in February-March. Grow rust-resistant varieties and spray Propiconazole or Tebuconazole if symptoms appear.
- Aphid (Tela): Attacks in February-March. Encourage natural predators like Ladybird beetles. Spray Imidacloprid only if population crosses Economic Threshold Level (ETL).

2. Rice Pests and Diseases:
- Rice Stem Borer (Tana ki Sundi): Causes dead hearts and white heads. Use Cartap Hydrochloride or Fipronil granules.
- Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB): Avoid excessive Urea and drain standing water for 3 to 4 days.

3. Cotton Pests (Sucking and Bollworms):
- Whitefly (Safaid Makhi): Causes leaf curl virus (CLCuV) and sooty mold. Use yellow sticky traps and spray Pyriproxyfen or Buprofezin.
- Pink Bollworm (Gulabi Sundi): Install pheromone traps (5 per acre) and spray Triazophos or Spinetoram at ETL.
- Jassid (Sabz Tela): Spray Nitenpyram or Dinotefuran when leaf curling appears on edges.
