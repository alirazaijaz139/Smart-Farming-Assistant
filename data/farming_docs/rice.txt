CROP: RICE / PADDY (CHAWAL / DHAN) - PAKISTAN AGRICULTURAL GUIDE

1. Nursery and Transplanting (Paneeri aur Muntaqili):
- Nursery sowing time for Basmati varieties is May 20 to June 20.
- Transplant 25 to 30 days old seedlings into puddled fields (Kaddoo).
- Maintain plant-to-plant and row-to-row distance of 9 inches (80,000 plants per acre).

2. Approved Varieties (Iqsam):
- Super Basmati, Basmati-515, Kisan Basmati, and PK-1121 Aromatic.
- Hybrid coarse varieties include Arize-6, Guard-53, and Y-26.

3. Water and Fertilizer Management:
- Keep 1.5 to 2 inches of standing water for the first 15 days after transplanting to control weeds.
- Apply 1.5 bags of DAP, 1.5 bags of Urea, and 1 bag of Potash per acre.
- Apply Zinc Sulphate (33%) at 5 kg per acre 10 to 12 days after transplanting to prevent Zinc deficiency.

4. Harvesting:
- Harvest when 80% to 85% of the grains in the panicle turn straw-colored.
